# Community rules / 社区规则

## English

We want Plyra discussions to be welcoming, useful, and safe for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

### Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Share context, credit creators, and distinguish personal opinion from project status.
- Keep posts relevant to Plyra and use the closest Discussion category.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's private information, credentials, or private messages without permission.
- Follow the licenses and rules that apply to work you share.

### Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and deliberately deceptive claims about released features are not acceptable. Do not use this community to distribute malware or to request unsafe handling of credentials.

### Moderation

Repository maintainers may hide or remove content, lock discussions, or restrict accounts when needed to protect the community. Moderation decisions should focus on behavior and safety. For a moderation concern, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub; do not publish sensitive personal details in a discussion.

## 简体中文

我们希望 Plyra 讨论区能够欢迎不同背景和经验水平的参与者，并保持有帮助、安全和尊重。

### 共同遵守

- 尊重他人并提供建设性意见。可以批评观点和代码，不攻击个人。
- 说明必要背景，尊重创作者署名，并区分个人观点与项目实际状态。
- 讨论应与 Plyra 相关，并选择最合适的分类。
- 尊重隐私。未经允许，不公开他人的个人信息、凭据或私人消息。
- 分享作品时遵守适用的许可证和规则。

### 不可接受的行为

骚扰、仇恨言论、威胁、人肉搜索、冒充他人、刷屏，以及故意将规划中的功能描述为已发布功能，均不可接受。不得借社区传播恶意软件或要求他人以不安全方式处理凭据。

### 管理方式

为保护社区，仓库维护者可以隐藏或移除内容、锁定讨论或限制账号。管理决定应聚焦行为和安全。如需反馈管理问题，请通过 GitHub 私下联系仓库维护者；不要在讨论中公开敏感个人信息。
